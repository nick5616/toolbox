Open-licence brushes bundled with Paint+
========================================

mypaint/   Brushes from the "mypaint-brushes" repository
           (https://github.com/mypaint/mypaint-brushes), converted to Paint+
           brush JSON by modules/paint/brushimport (see mypaint_library_test.go).
           Licence: CC0-1.0 for everything under brushes/ (Licenses.dep5:
           "By policy, MyPaint's brush settings are released into the public
           domain"). Authors, as listed there and carried in each brush's
           `credit`:
             - Martin Renold and the MyPaint Development Team (classic, experimental)
             - David Revoy (deevad)
             - Ramon Miranda (ramon)
             - Marcelo "Tanda" Cervino (tanda)
             - Guillaume Loussarevian (kaerhon_v1)
             - Brien Dieterle (Dieterle)
           Only the brush settings were used; no preview images or textures.

Not bundled: Krita's patterns and anything licensed "personal use only".
Users import those themselves (SPEC 1.3.2).
